Pick a moment in a video and get a 9:16 clip for TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels. Download it, or upload it to your own YouTube channel.

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Douyin. Only use videos you own or have permission to use.

Length

Only clips of up to 60 seconds can be uploaded to YouTube from here.

Leave blank to start from the beginning.

Needs ImageMagick installed. Without it the clip is made without text.

Downloading and rendering happen on your computer, so long clips can take a few minutes.

Uploading to YouTube is optional

To upload a finished short to your own channel, set up Google API credentials first (see "YouTube upload" in the README). Uploads start as private.
